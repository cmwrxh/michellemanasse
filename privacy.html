Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Michelle Manasse LLC, a marketing consultancy. You can contact us at michellemanasse1@gmail.com.

What we collect

This site has no accounts or forms, and it does not process payments itself. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to discuss possible work together.

Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-focused tool, to see how many people visit the site and which pages they view. Plausible does not use cookies and does not collect personal data or track you across other websites.

The home page also includes a tracking script from Whop, our payment platform. It helps us see which visits lead to purchases. It may collect usage information such as the pages you view and may use cookies or similar technologies, as described in Whop's own privacy policy.

Payments

Payments for our services are processed by Whop, a third-party platform. When you pay, Whop collects the payment and account details you provide to them, under their own terms and privacy policy. We do not see or store your full card details.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with third parties except service providers needed to run the site and email (such as Whop for payments, and our hosting and email providers), or where the law requires it.

Keeping your information

We keep email correspondence for as long as needed to respond to you and manage client work, then delete it when it is no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing the address above.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
